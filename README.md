# 🚀 Sky Doodles: Majestic Horizons! ☁️✈️

Welcome to **Sky Doodles**, the ultimate Luanti (Minetest) mod that brings your skies to life with autonomous, decorative airplanes! Transform your empty, silent skies into a bustling, dynamic horizon without lifting a finger.

Watch as these magnificent, lightweight planes soar above your world, drawing beautiful, fading white cloud trails across the sky. Perfectly balanced for ambiance, they bring a lively vibe to any server without any performance or interaction overhead.

## ✨ Incredible Features

- **Perfect Altitude Cruising:** Glide seamlessly at a fixed altitude of `y = 100`, designed to be perfectly visible for players exploring the surface!
- **Sleek & Lightweight:** Completely non-interactive and purely visual. They are a quarter of the size of traditional massive planes, making them look stunningly realistic from the ground!
- **Dynamic Cloud Trails:** Leaves gorgeous fading white contrails in their wake, adding breathtaking detail to your skies.
- **Smart Environmental Awareness:** Automatically despawns when far from players or if they tragically bump into a soaring mountain peak.
- **Optimized Spawning:** Smart clearance checks ensure planes only appear when the sky is clear and the light is right.

> **Note:** These planes are here for the view! They are NOT flyable, rideable, or interactive. If they crash or get too close to each other, they simply vanish to keep your world clean.
